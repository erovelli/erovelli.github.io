I work at Fidelity on full-stack engineering, mostly batch platforms and billing
systems. That work is where I learned to care about the things this site keeps
returning to: what happens when a job fails at 3am, how you make a pipeline
explain itself, and how much of a system's behaviour you can recover from its
logs six months later.

Outside of that I build local-first tools, usually in Rust.
[tidyup](/projects/tidyup/) organises files on-device instead of shipping them
to a model. [Outdegree](/projects/outdegree/) turns browser history into a graph
without asking for host permissions. Both started as things I wanted and could
not find a version of that I trusted.
